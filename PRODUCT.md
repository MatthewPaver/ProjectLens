# ProjectLens

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Project controls reviewers compare a project update with two Primavera P6 schedule exports, inspect changes and record a human decision. Portfolio visitors also need to understand the problem, try the real workflow and judge the engineering evidence. Practitioner demand and measured benefits remain unvalidated.

## Product Purpose

Make a schedule update's changes, supporting source evidence and outstanding review questions understandable before a person records a decision. The user confirmed that ProjectLens should open with a proper product landing page. It must explain the value before offering the working sample and the visitor's own-file journey.

## Positioning

A browser-local evidence-to-decision workflow: compare two XER exports, inspect their facts alongside the narrative, record a decision, export the record and track conditions. Do not claim that schedule comparison itself is novel, that the tool establishes causation or entitlement, or that it replaces Primavera P6 or professional judgement.

## Operating Context

The existing static product ships from docs/ on GitHub Pages. The synthetic Northstar pack demonstrates a 73-day scheduled finish movement. The guided walkthrough uses the real browser engine with disposable in-tab records. The ordinary route supports explicit local drafts, saved decisions, exports and conditions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two XER files and an optional narrative feed deterministic browser checks. Files and decision records stay in the browser during the standard workflow.
- Factual schedule changes must remain visible independently of narrative wording. Narrative checks are review prompts, not proof that an issue is resolved or that a pack is safe to approve.
- Preserve submitted and revised narrative text as distinct evidence when the narrative changes.
- A person supplies the decision, owner, rationale and any conditions. Context records are supplementary and require explicit human handling before inclusion.
- An optional, separately configured local research service can retrieve public records and produce a citation-checked AI brief. Its tracing support is optional; no live evaluation result or deployed AI capability may be inferred from configuration alone.
- Keep existing uploads, downloads, validation, browser storage, walkthrough isolation and decision/follow-up journeys working through the redesign.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the ProjectLens name. The user asks for Apple-level design care, taste and clear information hierarchy. The user explicitly rejects generic AI-generated website styling. Those references set a craft standard; they do not authorise copying another brand's identity.

## Evidence on Hand

The runnable product, source comparison engine, synthetic XER pairs, real-engine walkthrough, exported decision records, browser tests and existing before-and-after captures are available. Northstar facts include a 73-day finish movement, 10 materially changed activities, 3 constraint changes, 9 activities with reduced reported float and 5 added or removed predecessor signatures. These are demonstration facts, not customer outcomes.

No verified customer logos, testimonials, time savings, independent accuracy claim or production adoption evidence are available. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Explain what changed, show its source, state why it warrants review and identify the next question.
2. Keep observations, narrative claims, unresolved questions and human decisions distinct.
3. Demonstrate real behaviour with clearly labelled synthetic inputs.
4. State practical limits at the point where they affect interpretation.
5. Make product value understandable to a visitor without hiding the evidence from a practitioner. Lead with the practical finding and next review question; keep source files, check time and exact fields available through labelled disclosures.
