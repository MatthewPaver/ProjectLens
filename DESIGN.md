# Change assurance interface

Operate mode. Preserve ProjectLens's warm paper (#f1ecdf), near-black ink (#071a1b), off-white (#fffdf6), rust (#a94d35) and pale blue (#8eb0bd). Rust identifies unresolved evidence; green is reserved for the limited outcome of fixed checks. No causation, entitlement or approval claim follows from colour.

The review uses Libre Franklin: title 24px, body 14px, compact evidence 13px, metadata at least 12px. Archivo Black stays on the landing page. JetBrains Mono identifies source activity codes. Use 8/16/24/32px spacing, 1px borders, flat rectangular surfaces and no decorative gradients or shadows.

The top navigation remains Reviews, Decisions and Follow-up, with aria-current. The active review hides the landing pitch. Findings and source evidence share a two-column desk; a selected finding has a labelled pressed state. Mobile uses one pane and a Back to findings control which returns focus. The primary action records a human decision; questions and optional public context are secondary.

Keep raw source facts and the original narrative visible. Untouched supplemental records are not reviewed; using one never makes it an authoritative precedent. Owner and rationale are required. Conditional approvals require condition, owner and due date. Errors persist with recovery guidance. A deliberate device draft includes XER contents and form fields; saving and deletion are explicit. No automatic upload or localhost probing.

Focus uses a 3px blue outline, controls have visible hover and disabled states, and navigation transfers focus. Motion only scrolls to revealed work and respects reduced motion. Do not add app heroes, metric cards, invented confidence scores, decorative icons or sidebars.
