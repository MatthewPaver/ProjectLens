Read the method
Do my schedule files leave this browser?

The standard review reads your files locally. Saving a draft or decision stores it on this device. Downloads give you a copy to keep or share. Optional public-record research is separately configured and receives narrative text, never XER files.

Does a flagged change mean the project is delayed?

A later scheduled finish is an observed difference between exports. These checks do not recalculate the critical path or establish cause, contractual entitlement or the right decision.

What can I compare?

Two supported, single-project Primavera P6 XER exports from the same source project. ProjectLens checks their identity, dates and field coverage before comparing them. Missing fields and unsupported inputs are reported.
